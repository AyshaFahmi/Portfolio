# Aysha Fahmi — Engineering Portfolio

A personal portfolio showcasing my engineering background, optimisation research, digitalisation interests, and project work.

> “I connect the dots between engineering, data, operations, and people to make the whole system work better.”

## What’s inside

- **Route Lab:** Routing research and an interactive stop-selection demo
- **Systems in Practice:** Engineering and project examples
- **How I Connect:** My approach to understanding and improving systems
- **Research Desk:** Topics I’m exploring and writing about
- **My Perspective:** My background and roots in Kerala, India; currently based in Mladá Boleslav, Czech Republic
