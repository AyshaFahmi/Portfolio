01 / Featured project

Where mathematical models meet the real network.

My thesis is one example of how I connect different layers of a problem: its mathematical structure, available tools, and real-world context. Routing is one part of my portfolio, alongside systems design and operational coordination.

Master’s thesis · completed with honours

Use of Python in Routing Problems

The thesis developed a reusable Python mixed-integer optimisation framework for the Travelling Salesman Problem (TSP), TSP with Time Windows (TSPTW), Vehicle Routing Problem (VRP), and VRP with Time Windows (VRPTW). A separate Chinese Postman Problem (CPP) solver handles street-edge coverage, where the goal is to traverse every required link.

For an 11-customer case study, real-address locations were connected to road-network distances from OSRM and solved with Gurobi. The comparison showed the effect of vehicle capacity on total distance, while the specified time windows were non-binding for this dataset. The thesis received an excellent result.

03 / My approach

Optimisation starts before the algorithm.

A useful improvement begins with the right question and ends with evidence someone can act on.

01 — FRAME

Understand the system

Define the goal, the constraints, and what “better” means for the people using the process.

02 — EXPLORE

Model alternatives

Represent the problem, compare workable options, and make assumptions visible.

03 — VALIDATE

Measure the difference

Compare against a baseline, check trade-offs, and communicate what the evidence supports.
